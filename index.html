About

I am a communications professional with more than ten years of experience in strategic communications, public affairs, advocacy and editorial production. My career has moved between regional development, government and public diplomacy, and the common thread is the same: taking complex policy and technical subjects and making them clear to the people they affect.

Today I am Regional Communications Specialist at the Nile Equatorial Lakes Subsidiary Action Program Coordination Unit (NELSAP-CU), the investment arm of the Nile Basin Initiative. I lead communications for transboundary projects in hydropower, power interconnection, flood early warning, dam safety and water resources, advise senior leadership and coordinate communications across several countries.

Before that I spent a decade in public institutions: as Director of Communications at the Rwanda High Commission in Kampala, as Senior Public Relations Officer at the Rwanda Revenue Authority, and earlier as the High Commission's Communication Officer. I hold a Master of Arts in Digital Journalism from Aga Khan University, and I care about communication that is accessible and accountable.
